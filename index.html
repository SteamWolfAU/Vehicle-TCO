Novated Lease Detail (Year 1)

How it's calculated

  • Every option ends with you owning the car (balloon/residual paid), so all are compared on the same basis and the expected resale value is deducted.
  • Opportunity cost = interest each dollar would have earned from when it's spent until the end of the term.
  • Novated: financier claims GST on the price (capped at the car limit); employer claims GST on rentals & packaged costs. FBT is eliminated with post-tax Employee Contributions (ECM) unless the EV exemption applies. Base value drops by ⅓ from year 5.
  • Business use: running costs, interest, fees & lease rentals are deducted at your business-use %, with diminishing-value depreciation (25%) capped at the car limit. GST credits for GST-registered businesses and the luxury-car notional-loan rules for leases aren't modelled.
  • Tax uses resident rates, LITO, Medicare levy & (optional) surcharge. Estimates only — not financial or tax advice.
